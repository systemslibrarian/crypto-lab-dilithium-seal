Dilithium Seal

ML-DSA — NIST FIPS 204 — standardized from the CRYSTALS-Dilithium submission

Generate lattice-based keypairs, sign and verify messages, seal documents, and watch a single flipped bit fail verification.

WHY IT MATTERS

A quantum computer running Shor's algorithm would break RSA and ECDSA signatures. Shor's does not apply to lattice problems, so NIST standardized ML-DSA as the replacement: it is designed to keep signatures unforgeable against classical and quantum adversaries alike, resting on the assumption that Module-LWE and Module-SIS stay hard.
